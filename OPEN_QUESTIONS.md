# Open questions

The bots ask Dan the setup questions themselves (`skills/getting-started`). These are the decisions around them. The AI Co-Founder keeps this list current and puts the top unanswered one in the Friday session prep.

## Before setup

1. **GrokBot, Better than GrokBot, Hermes, or a mix?** On the Oct 21 call Dan chose to stay on Hermes. On Oct 24 he built more on it: his assistant, its GHL tools, the Slack move. This repo builds a GrokBot team that runs **next to** Hermes and replaces nothing until it's proven. Better than GrokBot (`INSTALL.md`) runs the same team from Dan's Mac, on Orgo computers like his Hermes. If Dan stays on Hermes, `docs/ON-HERMES.md` sets up the same agents as Hermes profiles.
2. **One account or two?** Business-only is enough to start. The personal account (Personal Assistant, Money Manager) can come later, or never. Meetings and reminders already work from the business account.
3. **Plan size.** Grok Bot needs Premium or higher. Milestone 1 runs about eight routines a day and fits Premium. Milestone 2 adds about ten more; Super or Ultra fits both.
4. **Rotate pasted secrets.** A Slack bot token and a GHL key were pasted into chats. Make new ones before setup.

## Milestone 1

5. **Is the funding business launched?** What counts as "launched": confirm or change the draft in `agents/05-funding-launch.md`.
6. **The funding project's status board.** Where it lives, so the Funding Launch bot reads it instead of asking again.
7. **Calendars and Zoom.** Which Google accounts Dan books from, and whether Zoom can be connected or a personal link is used.
8. **Priority people** for the ping scan, which workspaces to scan, and which channels to skip.
9. **VA digest.** Which channels the VA posts in that Dan wants summarized.
10. **Pricing, fulfilment, processing partner, counsel's "may say / never say"** for the funding business.

## Milestone 2 (when Dan restarts the EOD and income work)

11. **Is the Income Hub live?** If yes, the Revenue Analyst reads it. If not, each offer's comp structure and tracker link.
12. **Which Hermes EOD jobs are still running?** Dan paused EOD work on Oct 24. This decides what the preview compares against.
13. **Partner workspace install.** Who approves the Spike Ops install there? Or keep the Hermes Slack app; only one posts.
14. **EOD "@" and "Pace".** What are the two numbers around "@" on the MTD, Week and Today lines, and what is "Pace"? (The tracker shows sets @ closes and leaves Pace out until Dan says.)
15. **KPI targets** per closer and per setter for on track / yellow / behind.
16. **User groups.** Do @closers and @setters user groups exist? If yes, the roster is exact.
17. **Where the EOD summary goes** after cutover: Dan's DM (default) or a channel.
18. **Leaderboard in a channel?** Off by default; needs a standing approval for the exact format.
19. **Billing source** for the payment check: the billing sheet or Stripe (read-only key).

## Milestone 3 and later, each a separate decision

20. **Call recorders.** Which ones the team uses (Fathom, read.ai, Zoom, GHL), and whether the contractor will export the existing call database.
21. **Call rubric.** Keep the current grader's fields (default) or change them?
22. **CRM Coach.** When the HubSpot move happens, and whether GHL stays for the funding business.
23. **Reactivation Desk.** Whether to run dormant-lead reactivation as a standing job again.
24. **The Orgo computer.** Keep it as the Hermes home, or stop it once GrokBot has run a full month close.
25. **A separate funding agent.** Whether the funding business gets its own agent (or its own Orgo computer, if on Hermes).
