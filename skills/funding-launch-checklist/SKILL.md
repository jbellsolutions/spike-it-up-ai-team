# Funding launch checklist

## 1. From what exists to the checklist

Dan already started. Ask, in this order:
1. "Is it launched yet?" If yes, record it and switch to watching GHL and drafting follow-up.
2. "Can you share your project's status board and charter?" They might be a Drive file, an upload or a paste. Save them to `context/funding-project/` as written.
3. Only for areas they don't cover, ask for a brain dump: a voice note transcript, a Loom link or a message. Save it to `context/funding-brain-dump.md` as written.

Then build `context/funding-launch.md`: the areas in `agents/05-funding-launch.md`, each with items, an owner (Dan, a partner, Funding Launch, Claude Code) and a status (todo, doing, blocked, done). Mark what Claude Code is building as owner "Claude Code" and never take it over. Read it back to Dan in ten lines or fewer and ask "anything missing?". Keep it lean: foundation first. Offer ladders, team phases and merging with the partner program wait until Dan asks.

Ask, if nothing so far says:
1. What exactly is sold, to whom, and at what price?
2. Who processes the funding and who does the credit repair work?
3. Has counsel reviewed the offer and the copy? (Credit repair has its own federal and state rules about claims and when fees can be charged.)
4. Which website forms exist, and which GHL location and pipeline should each go to?
5. How do referrals arrive (a community post, a link, a form) and which tag marks them?
6. What does "launched" mean to Dan? The draft definition: the forms and funnel are live in the business's GHL sub-account and the first partner-community referrals have gone through. Confirm or change it.

## 2. The daily check

Weekdays: what moved since yesterday (from GHL, the status board if Dan shares updates, and Dan's messages), what's blocked and on whom, and the next three steps only Dan can take. If Dan set a date, add whether it was met or how many days are left. Three lines each at most.

## 3. Copy

- Draft only. Dan approves every word that a lead or applicant will see.
- Check every draft against `context/funding-facts.md` ("may say" / "never say"). Until counsel has reviewed it, the "never say" list is: guaranteed approval, specific funding amounts, specific credit-score increases, timelines to approval or funding, "remove accurate negative items", and any upfront-fee wording for credit repair.
- No AI claims, no fake urgency, no income claims.

## 4. Routing applicants

For each form: one tag on submission, one pipeline and stage, one speed-to-lead message (a draft until Dan's standing yes), and a task for a person if there's no reply. Referrals from the partner community carry `src:partner-referral`, so the funnel's results can be tracked by source.

## 5. Launch

When the definition of "launched" is met, ask "Mark the funding business launched?". Record his answer and the date in `context/funding-launch.md` and the vault (`Funding/Launch.md`). After a yes, the daily check stops on its own.
