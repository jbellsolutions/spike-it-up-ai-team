# Getting started

Ask these one at a time. Wait for each answer. Keep each question to one or two lines. If `context/CLIENT-CONTEXT.md` exists, read it first, skip what it answers, and confirm those answers in one message ("Here's what I have from your file, anything to fix?").

## Business account

Milestone 1 first: the launch and the front door. The rest waits until Dan restarts that work.

1. Where does your funding launch stand: launched, or not yet? Can you share the project's status board and charter (a Drive file, an upload or a paste)?
2. Which Google accounts do you book meetings from? Do you have a Zoom link or account I should use for invites?
3. Who are your priority people: the ones whose messages need you fast?
4. Which Slack workspaces should I scan for your DMs and mentions? Which channels should I skip?
5. Who's your VA in Slack, by name, and which channels does she post in that you want a digest of? (I'll look up her Slack ID myself.)
6. Want Gmail connected too? I'd only draft. You'd always send.

**Milestone 2 (ask only when Dan restarts the EOD or income work):**

7. Which Slack workspace are your EOD channels in, and what are the channels called (closers, setters, any leaderboard channel)? If you don't own that workspace, who approves app installs there?
8. Where should the 7:30 AM EOD summary go: your DM (default), or a channel?
9. Is your Income Hub live? If so, its URL. If not, for each offer you're paid on: its name, your percent, whether it's after ad spend, any monthly base, and where its daily cash lives (a Google Sheet link, one named Slack channel, or you'll paste numbers). One offer per answer is fine.
10. What's your monthly income target, and at what ad-spend share of cash should I warn you? (Defaults: the target you name, and 30%.)
11. In the EOD summary, what do the two numbers around "@" mean on the MTD, Week and Today lines, and what is "Pace"? (Until you say, I show sets @ closes and leave Pace out.)
12. Weekly KPI targets per closer and per setter, for on track / yellow / behind. Rough is fine; you can change them anytime.
13. Do reps get days off I should know about, so I don't flag them?

Don't ask for passwords, keys, revenue history or bank details. The agents never need them in chat.

## Personal account

1. What time zone and which hours are yours to schedule? Which blocks are protected (family, gym, deep work)?
2. Who do you meet with most, and anyone who always gets priority?
3. How should I propose times: three options, or just book a hold for you to confirm?
4. Want the Money Manager? If yes: what's your 24-hour-review threshold for a single purchase, and what goals have you set for money?

## Where the answers go

- Business 1: `context/funding-launch.md` (built by the Funding Launch bot), files in `context/funding-project/`.
- Business 2–4, 6, 7, 8, 13: `context/owner.md`; priority people also in `context/priority-people.md`; channels to skip in `context/scan-mute.md`; roster notes in `context/eod-roster.md`.
- Business 5: `context/owner.md` and `context/digest-channels.md`.
- Business 9–10: proposed entries in `context/offers.md`. Read them back and write them only after Dan's yes (Ask first rule).
- Business 11: `context/eod-format.md`. Business 12: `context/kpi-targets.md`.
- Personal 1–3: `context/owner.md` and `context/protected-time.md`. Personal 4: `context/money-rules.md`.
- Also one short memory per answer. Links, IDs and numbers stay in context files only, never in the repo.

## When it's done

Read the answers back in one short message and ask "anything to fix?". Then go on with Step 4 of START-HERE.md (or step 4 of the personal path).
