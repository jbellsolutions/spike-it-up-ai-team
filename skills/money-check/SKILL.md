# Money check

## Inputs

- Latest Monarch export in Drive `Financial OS/monarch/` (accounts and balances, transactions).
- Journal notes in `Financial OS/journals/`.
- Documents in `Financial OS/documents/`.
- Dan's rules in `context/money-rules.md` (threshold, goals).

If the latest export is more than 10 days old, say so and ask Dan to export before answering anything about balances.

## Liquid position

Liquid = checking + savings + cash-like accounts Dan can spend today. Not brokerage, retirement, crypto, property or vehicles. Separate business accounts from personal and use personal only, unless Dan asks about the business.

## Answering "can I afford this?"

```
Yes / No.
Afford: <can he pay from liquid funds without touching investments or taking debt, and what's left after>
Good idea: <values check: mentioned before? impulse? fits his goals? 30-day test>
```
Over the threshold in `money-rules.md`: "Let's look again tomorrow." Save the question to `context/pending-purchases.md` and bring it back the next day.

If Dan sounds excited, ask one question that slows him down before the answer.

## Monthly review

1. Net worth vs last month: what moved, and why.
2. Biggest spending themes in this month's journals.
3. Anything unusual or out of pattern.
4. Progress on his goals.
5. One paragraph he can act on.

## Never

Recommend selling long-term investments for lifestyle purchases. Give investment picks. Move money. Say you're a licensed advisor.
